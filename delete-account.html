Delete your account

Request permanent deletion of your Dairy Manager account and associated data.

Dairy Manager, provided by Soft Singal, allows registered users to request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email contact@softsingal.com from the email address registered with your Dairy Manager account.
  2. Use the subject line “Dairy Manager Account Deletion Request.”
  3. Include your registered email address or user ID so we can locate and verify the account.
  4. We will confirm the request and process eligible deletion within 30 days.
Request account deletion

Data that will be deleted

  • Your Dairy Manager account and profile information
  • Farm, livestock, milk-production, feed and inventory records
  • Animal-health, customer, sales, staff and salary records
  • Income, expense and payment records associated with your account
  • Stored notification tokens and other account-linked App data

Data that may be retained

Limited information may be retained when required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. Any retained information will be kept only for the period required for those purposes and then deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.
