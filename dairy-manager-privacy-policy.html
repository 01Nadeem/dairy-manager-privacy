Dairy Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026
Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dairy Manager (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the App.

The App is provided by Soft Singal. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@softsingal.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • User identifier
  • Authentication information managed securely by Firebase Authentication

We do not directly store or have access to your account password.

Farm and business records

The App allows you to enter and manage information such as:

  • Farm details
  • Animal and livestock records
  • Milk-production records
  • Feed and inventory records
  • Animal health records
  • Customer and sales records
  • Staff and salary records
  • Income, expense, and payment records

These are record-keeping features only. Dairy Manager does not process bank payments or money transfers.

Location information

With your permission, the App may access your approximate or precise device location to help fill in your farm address.

Location access is requested only when you use this feature. You may refuse or withdraw permission through your device settings.

Notification information

If you enable notifications, we may collect and store a device notification token to deliver reminders and app-related notifications.

Technical information

Our service providers may automatically process limited technical information, such as:

  • Device and operating-system information
  • App version
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Authentication and security logs

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide the App’s farm-management features
  • Store and synchronize records across supported devices
  • Display farm reports and calculations
  • Fill in farm-location information when requested
  • Deliver notifications enabled by the user
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability
  • Respond to support and data-deletion requests

3. Third-Party Services

Dairy Manager uses third-party services that may process information on our behalf.

These services include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication for user accounts
  • Google Cloud Firestore for storing and synchronizing App records
  • Firebase Storage for cloud-file storage where applicable
  • Expo services for building the App and delivering enabled push notifications

These providers process information according to their respective privacy policies and security practices.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Expo Privacy Policy

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Information may be shared only:

  • With service providers required to operate the App
  • When you direct or authorize us to share it
  • To comply with a legal obligation
  • To protect users, our rights, or the security of the App
  • During a business transfer, subject to appropriate privacy protections

5. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored using Google Firebase and related cloud infrastructure.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. However, no electronic storage or internet-transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting your login credentials and restricting unauthorized access to your device and account.

6. Data Retention

We retain account information and App records while your account remains active or as necessary to provide the App.

Information may also be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. When information is no longer required, we will delete or anonymize it where reasonably possible.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Dairy Manager account and associated personal data by emailing contact@softsingal.com.

Use the subject line “Dairy Manager Account Deletion Request” and send the request from the email address registered with your account.

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize eligible account information and associated records within 30 days, except information that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw location or notification permissions
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Contact us with a privacy complaint

Device permissions can be managed through your Android device settings.

9. Children’s Privacy

Dairy Manager is intended for dairy-farm owners, managers, and staff. It is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum legal age applicable in their country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. International Processing

Information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live. Our service providers apply safeguards according to their policies and applicable laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or legal requirements change.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or another appropriate method.
